# Journal-tracker
A habit and journal tracker that keeps a record of what I actually did.
I made it to hold myself to a 6-month promise. Every day I check off my habits, log my sleep and mood, and write a short note. The graphs show whether I'm really doing it.

[LIVE SITE](https://nikhilkshub.github.io/Journal-tracker/)

## What it does
- Check off your habits each day
- Log hours slept, mood (1-10) and a daily note
- Go back and fill in a day you missed
- Switch the view between Today, 7 days, 30 days and All time
- See your streak, best streak and the percentage of habits done
- A wall of the last weeks where each day gets brighter the more you finish
- Graphs for habits done per day, sleep and mood, and each habit on its own
- Add or remove your own habits
- Works on laptop and phone

A day counts toward your streak when you finish at least half of your habits.

## Privacy
Everything is saved in your own browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent to a server, and there is no login. Because of this, your data stays on the device and browser you used. Clearing your browser's site data will erase it.

## Run it yourself
It is one file. Download `index.html` and open it in a browser. No install needed.

## Built with
HTML, CSS and plain JavaScript. Fonts are Newsreader, Plus Jakarta Sans and JetBrains Mono from Google Fonts.
